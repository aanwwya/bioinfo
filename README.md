# bioinfo

a collection of python projects exploring the fundamentals of bioinformatics and dna sequence analysis.

this repository starts with small sequence-analysis programs and will gradually grow into a larger bioinformatics engineering project focused on efficient sequence processing, algorithms, performance, and scalability.

## projects

### 01 — dna sequence analyzer

analyzes a dna sequence by calculating its length, nucleotide counts, gc content, at content, validity, and complementary sequence.

### 02 — dna transcription

converts a dna sequence into its corresponding rna sequence and performs basic validation.

### 03 — reverse complement

generates the complementary and reverse complementary strands of a dna sequence.

### 04 — codon splitter

splits a dna sequence into groups of three bases called codons and identifies incomplete bases.

### 05 — protein translation

demonstrates how dna codons can be mapped to their corresponding amino acids.
